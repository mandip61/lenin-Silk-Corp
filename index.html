A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

        
Before we begin.

      

      

        

          How is my garment priced?
          
Pricing is quoted individually based on your selected garment, fabric and tailoring details. Share your
            preferences to start a conversation.

        

        

          Can I order from outside India or Germany?
          
Yes. Lenin Silk Corp offers worldwide delivery. Shipping arrangements, applicable charges and delivery
            timing will be discussed with your quote.

        

        

          How do I arrange my measurements?
          
Request a tailor appointment through the enquiry form. Our team will confirm the arrangements available for
            your location. You can also include existing measurements with their units.

        

        

          When will my garment be ready?
          
Your tailoring and delivery timeline will be confirmed with your quote, based on the garment, fabric and
            destination.
